Image optimization is crucial for web performance. In 2026, the two leading formats are AVIF and WebP. But which one should you choose?

## What is WebP?
WebP is a modern image format that provides superior lossless and lossy compression for images on the web. Developed by Google, it has been the industry standard for several years.

## What is AVIF?
AVIF (AV1 Image File Format) is the new kid on the block. It offers even better compression than WebP, often reducing file sizes by an additional 20-30% without sacrificing quality.

## Compression Efficiency
- **WebP:** Excellent for most use cases.
- **AVIF:** Superior for complex images with many details or gradients.

## Browser Support
As of 2026, both formats have near-universal support in modern browsers, including Chrome, Firefox, Safari, and Edge.

## Conclusion
For maximum performance, **AVIF** is the clear winner. However, **WebP** remains a solid choice with slightly faster encoding times. At MrCompress, we support both to give you the best of both worlds.
