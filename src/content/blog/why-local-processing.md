Every day, millions of people upload private photos to "free" online image converters. But have you ever wondered what happens to those images once they hit the server?

## The Hidden Risk of Cloud Converters
When you upload an image to a traditional cloud-based tool, you are sending your data to a remote server. This server might:
- Store a copy of your image indefinitely.
- Use your photos to train AI models without your consent.
- Be vulnerable to data breaches.

## The MrCompress Difference
MrCompress was built on a "Privacy First" philosophy. Instead of uploading your images to our servers, we bring the processing power to you.

### How it Works
Using modern web technologies like WebAssembly and specialized JavaScript libraries, we perform the compression and conversion directly in your browser.
- **No Uploads:** Your images never leave your computer.
- **Zero Latency:** Processing is limited only by your device's speed, not your internet connection.
- **True Privacy:** Even we can't see what you are optimizing.

## Conclusion
In an era of increasing digital surveillance and data harvesting, local processing isn't just a feature—it's a necessity. Choose tools that respect your privacy.
